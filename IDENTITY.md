# Community identity policy

## Purpose

The Horizontal Front needs a recognisable public identity so players can find the canonical project and tell who is accountable for an official release. It does not need an exclusionary brand strategy.

This policy is a community covenant and a rule for project-controlled spaces and assets. The project is not pursuing patents or registered trademarks. It does not promise that intellectual-property or consumer-protection law is irrelevant; it chooses not to make legal exclusion the centre of its identity.

## Canonical project

The canonical public home is `thehorizontalfront.org`. Official repositories, releases and community spaces must be identified from that site or from another channel recorded through the public governance process.

Only releases and communications approved through [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) speak officially for the project. Control of a similar domain, account, package name or fork does not confer that status.

## Uses the project welcomes

People may, consistently with the applicable copyright licences:

- Refer to The Horizontal Front truthfully.
- Discuss, review, criticise, teach with or report on it.
- Share screenshots and attributed excerpts.
- Make fan work, translations and adaptations.
- Fork and redistribute the software and cultural material, including commercially.
- State accurately that work is derived from or compatible with The Horizontal Front.

No permission or endorsement is needed for those uses. Criticism and political disagreement are not identity misuse.

## Avoiding confusion

Forks and adaptations should make their independent status prominent and should not claim endorsement, governance authority or official status they do not have. When there is a realistic risk of confusion, use a distinct release name and visual identity while retaining honest attribution to The Horizontal Front.

Do not use project identity to impersonate maintainers, misdirect donations, conceal the source of a release or falsely suggest that modified content represents the canonical project.

These requests protect community accountability; they do not add restrictions to AGPL- or CC-licensed material. The project will respond first with clarification, dialogue and visible provenance rather than threats.

## Logos and identity artwork

Original logos and identity artwork are cultural works licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 unless their asset records say otherwise. Reuse and adaptation are permitted under that licence. A modified logo must not be presented in a way that falsely implies an independent release is official.

Identity-critical artwork should retain the provenance and human-review safeguards in `AGENTS.md`.

## Patents and registration

The project will not seek patents over its software, mechanics, formats or creative methods.

The project does not currently intend to register a trademark. If demonstrable impersonation or enclosure later makes registration appear necessary to protect the commons, that would require a public material proposal under the charter. Any protection considered must be narrowly defensive, community-accountable and accompanied by a broad public-use policy. Registration must never become a mechanism for suppressing forks, criticism, political speech or good-faith cultural participation.

## Changing this policy

Changes follow the material-decision process in `GOVERNANCE.md` and cannot narrow the freedoms granted by the project's licences or weaken the project charter.
