# Delivered

This is what The Horizontal Front has finished. The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) holds
what is ahead; work leaves it and arrives here when it is done.

It is not a release history. [The release
records](docs/releases/README.md) say what shipped in each version, and much of
the work below shipped in no release at all: the participation obligations were
weeks of repository process that changed nothing a player can see.

Read this page as an answer to "has this project done anything?", which is a
different question from "what is it doing next?" — and the reason the two are
not on the same page.

## How work arrives here

A tranche retires when all of it is done, and brings its children with it. A
tranche with one unfinished child stays on the roadmap listing all of them,
because that is its scope. Work separate from the tranches retires when it
closes, having no tranche to wait for.

Nothing is deleted. Both pages are generated from one record in the repository,
and a check verifies that everything here is closed and that nothing still on
the plan is entirely finished.

Commitment values are not repeated here. *Committed* and *wanted, not
scheduled* describe an intention, and these are outcomes.
