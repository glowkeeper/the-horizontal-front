## Boundaries and exceptions

- Third-party dependencies retain their own licences.
- Third-party or commissioned assets retain the licence recorded for each asset; they are not relicensed merely by appearing in this repository.
- Generated and procedurally produced material may be distributed only to the extent that the project has the rights required to do so. Preserve its provenance record.
- Names and other indications of official project identity are addressed by the [community identity policy](IDENTITY.md). That policy does not remove permissions granted by the licences above.
- No patent licence or patent application is created by the community identity policy. Patent terms contained in an applicable software licence continue to apply.

When the category of a file is genuinely ambiguous, record an explicit SPDX identifier in that file or its asset-manifest entry rather than guessing.
