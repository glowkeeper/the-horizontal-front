# Research

Research notes record the evidence considered before a design decision, the
limits of that evidence and the inferences made specifically for The Horizontal
Front. They remain separate from product and architecture documents so a concise
summary cannot silently turn a tentative finding into a universal claim.

## Current notes

- [Interruption mechanics and accessible rhythm
  UI](interruption-mechanics-and-accessible-rhythm-ui.md) — phrase-boundary
  interruption timing, warning and recovery, input cancellation, multimodal
  rhythm cues and the resulting Quick Call and Urgent Email constraints.
- [Political satire and radical print art
  direction](political-satire-art-direction.md) — political-cartoon techniques,
  radical print traditions, five complementary reference practices, their
  limitations and the resulting first-production-draft hypotheses.
- [First production concept sheet](first-production-concept-sheet.md) — the
  initial unedited visual test, complete generation provenance and source-level
  questions for maintainer review before a targeted second draft.
- [Second production concept sheet](second-production-concept-sheet.md) — a
  quieter line-art comparison preserving the first sheet while testing
  editorial economy, paper space and a single Management presence.
- [Third production concept sheet](third-production-concept-sheet.md) — a
  direct return to the preferred first sheet that preserves its protagonist and
  grotesquerie while selectively reducing spectacle and clutter.
- [Fourth production concept sheet](fourth-production-concept-sheet.md) — a
  constrained Management redesign testing pompous administrative comedy while
  moving genuine threat into the coercive machinery.
- [Fifth production concept sheet](fifth-production-concept-sheet.md) — an
  expression-state iteration testing wounded vanity and comic corporate rage in
  response to the player's defiance.

Research informs decisions but does not override the project charter, the
implemented episode grammar or observed playtesting results.
