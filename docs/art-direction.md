# Art direction

## Status

This is the provisional art direction for the first production draft. It is a
deliberate brief rather than final perceptual acceptance: asset production,
integration and maintainer playtesting may reveal changes that should be
recorded here before the first production release is accepted.

## Political and visual purpose

The game uses original comic political satire to make a modest demand appear
with the heroic scale it deserves:

> Let me remain in bed.

The humour comes from the contrast between the player's clear, reasonable and
defiant refusal and Management's increasingly excessive machinery of compulsory
productivity. The scene must not read as a generic sleep, wellness or laziness
story. Remaining horizontal is an active political stance.

Historical political cartoons may inform the use of symbolism, exaggeration,
irony, silhouette and theatrical composition. They are research references,
not styles to imitate. Production prompts and briefs must not request imitation
of a living artist, a named entertainment property or one historical artist's
recognisable rendering style.

## Reference traditions

The first production draft draws inspiration from a deliberate combination of
left-wing, radical and anti-authoritarian graphic traditions. These references
identify political and visual qualities to study; they are not a request to
copy a particular work or reproduce any artist's recognisable style.

- **Emory Douglas — heroic political authority.** Study the monumental scale,
  strong posture, limited colour and unequivocal dignity given to ordinary
  people engaged in resistance. This principally informs whom the composition
  honours: the player is an active political subject, not an object of pity.
  See the [National Gallery of Art overview and selected
  works](https://www.nga.gov/artists/35198-emory-douglas).
- **David Low — readable theatrical satire.** Study the economical staging,
  immediately legible central joke, symbolic props and ability to make
  authoritarian power dangerous and ridiculous at once. This principally
  informs how the confrontation reads as a scene. See the [British Cartoon
  Archive biography and holdings](https://research.kent.ac.uk/british-cartoon-archive/record/david-low/).
- **Clifford Harper — oppositional print character.** Study the handmade,
  woodcut-like energy, strong black shapes and visual language of labour,
  anarchist and collective action. This may inform texture, line, poster-like
  outcome treatment and interface ornament without making the warm bedroom
  uniformly severe. See the artist's [biography and account of his
  work](https://cliffordharper.com/biography/).
- **Käthe Kollwitz — bodily conviction and dignity.** Study the expressive
  hands, physical weight and monumental treatment of workers and ordinary
  people. This may inform the player's mass, determination and resistance poses
  without importing the predominantly sombre emotional register of Kollwitz's
  work. See MoMA's discussion of [Kollwitz's working-class
  subjects](https://www.moma.org/magazine/articles/1051).
- **Steve Bell — grotesque managerial excess.** Study the willingness to make
  political power bodily, recurring, rude and absurd. This may give Management
  permission to become genuinely grotesque, but the Orange Fella must not use
  Bell's recognisable drawing manner or depend on caricaturing a real person's
  face. See the artist's [cartoon archive](https://www.belltoons.co.uk/bellworks/).

The intended synthesis is a heroic radical-print protagonist occupying a warm
horizontal stronghold while an editorial-cartoon apparatus of managerial power
invades from above and the right. Douglas principally informs whom the image
honours; Low informs how the joke reads; Harper and Kollwitz may inform physical
mark and bodily conviction; Bell gives Management permission to become
grotesquely undignified. The resulting artwork must nevertheless establish its
own shapes, characters, line, palette, compositions and recurring visual jokes.

## The player is the hero

The player character is heroic, defiant and purposeful while remaining in bed.
They are not principally timid, helpless or vulnerable. The bed is a defended
position and the player is the composition's centre of moral authority even
when Management occupies more space or applies more force.

Express that direction through:

- a broad, stable horizontal silhouette;
- a firm gaze, purposeful hands and an immovable planted posture;
- warm, human and rumpled forms that remain strong rather than diminished;
- clear resistance poses for duvet pulling, mattress gripping and sustained
  holds;
- readable determination at gameplay scale; and
- occasional restrained banner- or cape-like movement in the duvet without
  turning the character into a generic superhero.

Danger and loss must remain legible, but exposure does not remove the player's
agency or make humiliation the joke. Forced verticalisation should feel funny,
dramatic and sad because power has imposed an absurd demand, not because the
player was foolish to resist it.

## Management

The Orange Fella is a fictional political and corporate archetype, never a
portrait or close likeness of a real person. He may be materially imposing but
should remain ridiculous, entitled and spiritually small beside the player's
conviction.

Management's visual language uses invasive scale, orange-gold excess, sterile
corporate finish, grasping limbs, hard light, paperwork and office machinery.
Its narrow vertical intrusions oppose the player's stable horizontal mass.
Power is communicated through what Management does to the room, not through a
recognisable public figure's face.

## Shape, line and colour

The first production draft should explore:

- slightly rough ink contours and deliberately imperfect cut-paper forms;
- strong silhouettes and sparse detail where gameplay must remain readable;
- the existing semantic palette rather than ad hoc asset colours;
- warm, soft, lived-in bedroom shapes against hard managerial geometry;
- richer incidental jokes in briefings and outcomes, away from critical input
  moments; and
- deliberately layered cut-out parts prepared for pivots, duvet movement,
  mattress lift, Management's arm and expressive pose changes.

Every important state must remain distinguishable without colour alone. Text,
rhythm targets, hands, faces and movement paths need sufficient contrast and
clear space at supported desktop and landscape-mobile sizes.

## Production boundaries

Artwork is selected through the validated asset catalogue and ownership-scoped
skins. Assets must be created at sufficient resolution, separated into the
parts their animation role requires and recorded with complete provenance.

The first production draft must be reviewed for recognisable characters,
likenesses, logos, branding, signatures, watermarks, suspiciously specific
similarities and generation artefacts. Identity-critical assets—especially the
player, Orange Fella and public identity—require meaningful maintainer art
direction and human refinement before production approval.
