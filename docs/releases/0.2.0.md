# 0.2.0 — The Monday Uprising

Status: first public release, not a production release

The first public release of The Horizontal Front: a satirical rhythm game about working extremely hard to remain in bed, while Management tries to force you into work.

Play it at [thehorizontalfront.org](https://thehorizontalfront.org).

**This is a public release, not a production release.** It is playable, complete and honest about what it is missing. `1.0.0` is reserved for a release meeting the [no-placeholders invariant](../release-process.md), including human authorship of identity-critical artwork. What is missing is listed at the bottom, not buried.

## What is in it

One campaign, **The Monday Uprising**, containing one episode, **The Alarm**.

You hold the line to a rhythm played on the apparatus of the workplace being satirised — rubber stamps keep the beat, a typewriter answers it, a jammed key is a miss. Management hauls on a brass lever bolted to the head of your bed, and two interruptions arrive to break your rhythm: **Just a Quick Call**, which wants three excuses in the correct order, and an **Urgent Email**, which must be marked unread before it opens.

Win and the line holds. Lose and you are forced vertical.

The episode format contains no code. An episode is structured data, writing, artwork and audio drawn from a documented vocabulary, which is what will let other people write other workplaces.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** left and right arrows for the two sides, held for long notes; Enter and Escape to move between screens; number keys during Quick Call; Space to hold; R to retry an outcome.
- **Pointer:** every control is clickable, including the in-world resistance controls.
- **Touch:** the same controls, sized to be hit with a thumb.

Sound can be muted at any time. Muted play is mechanically identical and communicates timing, state, interruptions and outcomes without audio.

## Supported environments

Verified, and claimed only for what was verified:

- Desktop Google Chrome, keyboard and pointer, at 1280x720.
- Google Chrome 151.0.7922.137 on a Motorola Moto G84 5G running Android 15, touch input, in landscape and portrait. Landscape is the better experience.
- Audio-enabled play and muted play.

Other browsers, devices and Android versions may work. They are not claimed.

## Accessibility

- Interface controls are real DOM buttons layered over the canvas, keyboard-operable and exposed to assistive technology.
- Focus is placed deliberately on each screen and never dropped to the document body between scenes.
- A live region reports navigation, interruption and gameplay state.
- Timing is communicated visually and audibly, and each demand is announced before it falls due, so the score can be followed by ear as well as read on screen.
- Hit, miss, rest and hold states are distinguished by text as well as colour.
- Controls meet the WCAG 2.2 enhanced 44px target.
- Reduced-motion preferences shorten or remove animation without removing information.

**Not evaluated:** mobile screen readers, including TalkBack. No assistive-technology support is claimed beyond the above.

## Offline

The complete release is static. Once its files are cached, play does not depend on a backend, account, database, third-party API or any remote service remaining available.

## Licences and provenance

- Software: **AGPL-3.0-or-later**
- Cultural and documentary work: **CC-BY-SA-4.0**

Every shipped asset is catalogued with its origin, creator, generation tool where applicable, edits and licence.

**An honest caveat about the artwork.** The illustrations and character art are AI-generated under maintainer direction, then integrated by hand. The US Copyright Office's 2025 report indicates that this does not by itself produce protectable human authorship, and the UK fallback under CDPA s.9(3) is now proposed for removal. CC-BY-SA-4.0 is applied to them in good faith, but it may grant less than it appears to, and anyone relying on that should know. The favicon and site mark are original vector geometry composed by the maintainer and are not affected.

This is the specific gap between this release and a production one, and the reason it is numbered `0.2.0`.

## Known limitations

- The artwork is AI-generated, with the copyright standing described above. Human refinement of Management and the protagonist is wanted and not done.
- The audio mix is a first pass, tuned by a maintainer working without a sound designer.
- Playtesting is one person, not representative players. Real playtesting starts now.
- Mobile screen readers are unevaluated.
- One Android device and one Chrome version were tested.
- Management's console is drawn slightly differently in each of the four poses, so nominally fixed apparatus shifts as they strain.
- Authoring a new episode currently means writing JSON by hand against the grammar reference. It is documented and possible; it is not yet pleasant. An authoring tool is planned and not built.
