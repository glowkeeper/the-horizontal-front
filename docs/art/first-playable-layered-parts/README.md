# First-playable layered parts

These are the separated cut-out parts produced for the first-draft enactment of
`The Alarm`: protagonist and Management components, bed frame, mattress, pillows
and duvet states, plus the two whole-figure gameplay poses and the original
prototype pillow.

## Why they are here rather than in the runtime catalogue

Integrated review found that rotating a protagonist, duvet, mattress and bed
independently produced incompatible perspective. The accepted replacement is a
set of complete authored bed tableaux sharing one perspective plane, which is
what the episode skin now selects.

These parts are retained as visual development material. They are deliberately
outside `src/play/content/presentation/assets/`, so they are not catalogued, not
preloaded, not emitted into the production build and not written into the
offline cache — a player never downloads them. Their provenance is recorded in
the art documents that reference them.

They remain available if limited cut-out animation is revisited, in which case
they would need re-cataloguing with provenance, human refinement and perceptual
acceptance before shipping.
